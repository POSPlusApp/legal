POSplus

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

POSplus is a point-of-sale system used by bar and venue staff to manage tabs, orders, and payments. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information we collect

POSplus is used by venue staff, not directly by the general public. The information it handles falls into a few categories:

  • Guest tab information — a customer's name (optional — a tab can be opened with no name at all), table number, and order history for that visit. Guests are not required to create an account or provide any information beyond what they choose to give staff.
  • Order and payment details — items ordered, prices, and which payment method was used (cash, card, or QR code). POSplus does not process card payments directly and does not collect or store card numbers, expiry dates, or CVV codes — payment method is recorded only as a label for the bar's own records.
  • Staff account information — a name and a PIN code used to identify who placed or actioned an order, for internal accountability and reporting.
  • VIP guest lists — names and optional notes that bar management choose to save about recurring guests, entered directly by staff.
  • Venue/business details — the bar's own name, address, and configuration, set up by the bar's management.

Loyalty app integration

Some venues also offer a companion loyalty app to their customers. Where a customer chooses to use that app, their account details, order history, and loyalty points are stored to provide that service, and are visible to venue staff through POSplus for the purpose of fulfilling that customer's order. This is entirely separate from, and additional to, the guest tab information described above.

How information is used

Information collected through POSplus is used only to:

  • Take and manage customer orders and tabs
  • Process and record payments at the venue
  • Generate receipts and internal sales reports for the venue
  • Let venue management track staff performance, such as which drinks were poured on the house or for promotional purposes

We do not sell any information collected through POSplus, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where information is stored

Data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and backend service. Each venue's data is kept logically separate from other venues using the same system.

Data retention

Order and receipt records are retained by the venue for their own accounting and reporting purposes. A venue's management can request deletion of records they control by contacting us using the details below.

Your rights

If you are a customer of a venue using POSplus and have questions about information a venue holds about you, please contact that venue directly, or reach out to us using the details below and we will help direct your request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
